# Honda Maintenance and Parts Cost Estimates (Pakistan)
Source: Bloom Pakistan Honda maintenance guide (https://bloompakistan.com/?p=83896); Bloom Pakistan Honda City 2025 (https://bloompakistan.com/?p=46296)

These are estimated ranges, not fixed dealer prices.

**Honda service in Pakistan (Bloom Pakistan):**
- Recommended service interval: every 5,000 km or 3 months
- Engine oil and filter change at a dealership: Rs 8,000 to Rs 12,000
- Brake service: Rs 10,000 to Rs 20,000

**Honda City parts and service (Bloom Pakistan, 2025):**
- Oil change: Rs 2,500 to Rs 4,000
- Brake pads change: Rs 7,000 to Rs 12,000
- Air filter replacement: Rs 1,500 to Rs 2,500

Dealership prices are higher than independent workshops but include genuine parts and keep the warranty valid. Confirm with an Atlas Honda authorised dealer.
