Popular 2.8-litre diesel double-cab pickup used for both work and family. The Rocco is the sporty variant.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 12,329,000 to Rs 14,869,000.

- V Automatic 2.8: Rs 14,279,000, 2755 cc, diesel, automatic, 5 seats
- Rocco: Rs 14,869,000, 2755 cc, diesel, automatic, 5 seats
