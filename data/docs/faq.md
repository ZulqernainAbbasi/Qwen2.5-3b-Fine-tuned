# Frequently Asked Questions
Source: Project team FAQ based on the cars.csv dataset (Nov 2026)

**Which brands does this assistant cover?** Toyota, Honda, Suzuki, KIA, Hyundai, Changan and MG: new cars sold in Pakistan.

**Are prices on-road prices?** No. They are ex-factory prices as of November 2026. Registration, withholding tax, freight and insurance are extra. See the buying-costs guide.

**Which is the cheapest car in the dataset?** The Suzuki Every VXR (about Rs 2.97 million) and the Suzuki Alto VXR (about Rs 2.99 million).

**Which electric cars are covered?** MG4 EV Urban, MG Binguo EV and MG ZS EV.

**Which hybrids are covered?** Toyota Corolla Cross HEV, Honda HR-V e:HEV, Hyundai Elantra, Tucson and Santa Fe hybrids, MG HS hybrids, MG ZS Hybrid+ and the Suzuki Fronx GLX (mild hybrid).

**Can I book a test drive?** Yes. Give your name, a Pakistani mobile number (03XXXXXXXXX), the model, your city and a date, and the assistant books it after you confirm.

**What if I ask about a car that isn't in the data?** The assistant says it doesn't have verified information instead of guessing.

**Do prices change?** Often. Pakistani carmakers revised prices several times in 2025 and 2026 because of budgets, taxes and currency changes. Always confirm with an authorised dealer.
