# General Car Maintenance Guide (Pakistan conditions)
Source: Owner's-manual guidance summarised by the project team

- **Engine oil:** change every 5,000–10,000 km depending on the oil type (mineral vs synthetic) and your manufacturer's schedule. Dusty roads and stop-go traffic count as "severe conditions", so use the shorter interval.
- **Air filter:** inspect every 5,000 km in dusty cities and replace when dirty.
- **First free service:** most brands give the first one or two services free at 1,000 km and/or 5,000 km. Check your warranty booklet.
- **Hybrids:** need the same routine servicing as petrol cars plus hybrid system checks at authorised dealers. The hybrid battery has its own warranty terms.
- **EVs:** no oil changes. Mainly tyre, brake fluid, coolant and battery health checks.
- **Tyre pressure:** check monthly and before long trips, especially in summer heat.
- **Always follow your manufacturer's official service schedule.** It overrides this general guide.
