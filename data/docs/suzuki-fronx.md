# Suzuki Fronx
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/new-cars/suzuki/fronx/

Pakistan's first locally assembled Suzuki crossover (launched 6 May 2026) with a 1.5-litre K-series engine. The top GLX trim adds mild-hybrid tech, 360 camera and cruise control.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 5,999,999 to Rs 7,075,000.

## Variants
- GL MT: Rs 5,999,999, 1462 cc, petrol, manual, 5 seats, 14-22 km/l. Features: 6 airbags, rear AC vents, ABS, EBD
- GLX AT: Rs 7,075,000, 1462 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats, 14-22 km/l. Features: 6 airbags, 360-degree camera, cruise control, 9-inch infotainment, mild hybrid
