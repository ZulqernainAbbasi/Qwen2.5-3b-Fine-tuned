# Suzuki Cultus
Source: https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/08-Aug-2026/suzuki-cultus-new-price-installment-plans-in-pakistan-2026

1.0-litre five-door hatchback, a step up from the Alto in space and power. AGS (auto gear shift) variant available.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 4,089,490 to Rs 4,591,460.

## Variants
- VXR: Rs 4,089,490, 998 cc, petrol, manual, 5 seats
- VXL: Rs 4,359,160, 998 cc, petrol, manual, 5 seats
- VXL AGS: Rs 4,591,460, 998 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats. Features: auto gear shift
