# MG ZS
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/new-cars/mg/zs/

MG's compact SUV, offered as a petrol (Vibe) and as a Hybrid+.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 6,599,000 to Rs 7,499,000.

## Variants
- Vibe: Rs 6,599,000, 1498 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats, 10-13 km/l
- Hybrid+ Essence: Rs 7,499,000, 1498 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats. Features: 6 airbags, panoramic sunroof, rear AC vents, push start, lane keep assist, blind spot detection
