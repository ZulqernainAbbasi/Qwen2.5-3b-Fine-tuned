# Hyundai Tucson
Source: https://propakistani.pk/2026/08/12/hyundai-announces-massive-increase-in-hybrid-car-prices/

Fourth-generation Tucson, sold as a 1.6 turbo hybrid in Smart FWD and Signature AWD trims.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 12,202,000 to Rs 13,300,000.

## Variants
- Hybrid Smart FWD: Rs 12,202,000, 1598 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats, 14-17 km/l
- Hybrid Signature AWD: Rs 13,300,000, 1598 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats, 14-17 km/l. Features: all-wheel drive
