# Honda City
Source: https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/?p=332728

Honda's compact sedan. Honda keeps the base City under Rs 5 million so it stays in the lower sales-tax bracket (18% instead of 25%).

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 4,737,000 to Rs 6,149,000.

## Variants
- 1.2L CVT: Rs 4,737,000, 1199 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
- 1.5L Aspire CVT: Rs 6,069,000, 1498 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
- 1.5L Aspire S CVT: Rs 6,149,000, 1498 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
