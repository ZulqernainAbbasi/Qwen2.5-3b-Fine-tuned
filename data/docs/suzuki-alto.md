# Suzuki Alto
Source: https://www.bolnews.com/business/alto-cultus-swift-latest-prices-and-withholding-taxes-for-2026

Pakistan's best-selling entry-level 660cc city hatchback. Known for low running cost, cheap parts, a large dealer network and quick resale. Updated in April 2025 with airbags and ABS.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 2,994,861 to Rs 3,326,446.

## Variants
- VXR: Rs 2,994,861, 658 cc, petrol, manual, 4 seats, 16-22 km/l. Features: 2 airbags, ABS, power steering
- VXR AGS: Rs 3,166,480, 658 cc, petrol, automatic, 4 seats, 16-22 km/l. Features: 2 airbags, ABS, auto gear shift
- VXL AGS: Rs 3,326,446, 658 cc, petrol, automatic, 4 seats, 16-22 km/l. Features: 2 airbags, ABS, 6-inch display, power windows, power mirrors, air conditioner
