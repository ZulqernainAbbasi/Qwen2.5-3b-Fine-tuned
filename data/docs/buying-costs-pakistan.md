# Buying a New Car in Pakistan: Costs Beyond the Ex-Factory Price
Source: https://pakobserver.net/?p=780541

The prices in this assistant are ex-factory prices (Karachi). The on-road cost is higher because of:

- **Withholding / advance income tax (AIT):** collected at registration. Non-filers pay roughly three times what filers pay. For example, the Suzuki Alto VXR-AGS has about Rs 15,832 AIT for a filer and about Rs 47,497 for a non-filer (2026). The Swift GL is about Rs 66,902 for a filer and Rs 200,707 for a non-filer.
- **Registration fee and token tax:** set by your province and based on engine size or price.
- **Freight charges:** Toyota charges freight by city. In 2026, for the Corolla, Cross and Yaris, it was about Rs 30,000 in Karachi/Hyderabad, Rs 59,000 in Sukkur, Rs 96,000 in Multan/Quetta and Rs 125,000 for northern cities. Fortuner, Hiace and Camry freight ranged from Rs 37,000 (Karachi) to Rs 148,000 (north).
- **Insurance:** usually 1.5–3% of the car's value per year.
- **Sales tax bracket:** under Budget 2026-27, cars priced above Rs 5 million attract 25% sales tax instead of 18%. That's why some models (like the Honda City 1.2) are priced just under Rs 5 million.

Always confirm the final invoice with an authorised dealer.
