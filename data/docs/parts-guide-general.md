# Car Parts and Service Guide (all brands)
Source: Toyota Indus genuine parts guidance (https://toyota-indus.com/?p=2865) and PakWheels owner tips (https://www.pakwheels.com/new-cars/suzuki/alto/)

What the common service parts do and when to replace them. Always follow your owner's manual first.

- **Engine oil and oil filter:** lubricate and clean the engine. Change about every 5,000 km (or 3 months) in Pakistani city traffic and dust. The oil filter is replaced at every oil change.
- **Engine air filter:** stops dust entering the engine. In dusty cities, clean it often (PakWheels suggests every 3,000 km for the Alto) and replace it about every 10,000 km or when dirty.
- **Cabin (AC) filter:** cleans the air inside the car. Replace yearly or when AC airflow smells or weakens.
- **Spark plugs:** ignite the fuel. Typically replaced every 20,000 to 40,000 km for normal plugs (longer for iridium plugs).
- **Brake pads and brake fluid:** brake pads are checked at every service and replaced when worn; brake fluid usually every 2 years.
- **Coolant:** prevents overheating. Typically changed every 40,000 to 50,000 km.
- **Gear (transmission) oil:** manual and automatic/CVT gearboxes need different oils; follow the manual's interval.
- **Clutch plate (manual cars only):** wears with use, replaced when slipping.
- **Wiper blades:** replace when they streak, usually yearly.
- **Hybrid and electric cars:** need hybrid-system or battery checks at authorised dealers, in addition to normal servicing. EVs need no oil changes.

**Genuine vs copy parts:** Toyota notes that genuine parts are only available at authorised dealerships, and that parts sold well below market price are never genuine. Copy parts can look identical but filter poorly and can damage the engine. Using genuine parts at an authorised workshop also protects the warranty.

Parts prices vary by city, dealer and stock. The assistant only quotes part prices from its sourced price sheets (Suzuki, Toyota and Honda). For other brands, ask an authorised dealer.
