# Suzuki Swift
Source: https://www.bolnews.com/business/alto-cultus-swift-latest-prices-and-withholding-taxes-for-2026

Suzuki's most premium hatchback in Pakistan, with a 1.2-litre engine and a CVT option. Sportier styling than the Cultus.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 4,460,160 to Rs 4,766,190.

## Variants
- GL: Rs 4,460,160, 1197 cc, petrol, manual, 5 seats
- GL CVT: Rs 4,605,600, 1197 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
- GLX CVT: Rs 4,766,190, 1197 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats. Features: two-tone option
