# Toyota Yaris
Source: https://www.toyota-indus.com/yaris-booking

Toyota's entry sedan in Pakistan, positioned below the Corolla. Offered with 1.3 and 1.5-litre engines in manual and CVT.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 4,649,000 to Rs 6,389,000.

## Variants
- GLi MT 1.3: Rs 4,649,000, 1329 cc, petrol, manual, 5 seats
- GLi CVT 1.3: Rs 4,809,000, 1329 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
- ATIV MT 1.3: Rs 4,829,000, 1329 cc, petrol, manual, 5 seats
- ATIV CVT 1.3: Rs 5,719,000, 1329 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
- ATIV X CVT 1.5: Rs 6,389,000, 1496 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats. Features: beige interior
