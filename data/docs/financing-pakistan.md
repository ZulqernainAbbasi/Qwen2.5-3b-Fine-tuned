# Car Financing in Pakistan: Banks and SBP Rules
Source: SBP prudential regulations (https://www.sbp.org.pk/bprd/2021/CL29.htm); Express Tribune (https://tribune.com.pk/story/2358213/entertainment); bank comparison 2026 (https://www.toolsfluent.com/blog/car-loan-pakistan-2026-complete-guide); SBP KIBOR sheet 4 Sep 2026 (https://www.sbp.org.pk/assets/document/kibor-rates-04-september-2026.pdf)

**State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) rules for car loans (in force, Nov 2026):**
- Minimum down payment: 30% of the car's value.
- Total car financing per person, from all banks combined: at most Rs 3,000,000. For a car above Rs 4.3 million, the down payment must cover everything above Rs 3,000,000.
- Maximum loan period: 5 years for cars up to 1,000cc, 3 years for cars above 1,000cc.
- A draft government policy (AIDEP 2026-31, May 2026) proposes 7-year loans, a 15% down payment and a Rs 10 million cap for local cars, but it is not law yet.

**Banks offering car financing (indicative 2026 rates; banks set the final rate):**
- Meezan Bank, Car Ijarah (Islamic): about 14% to 17% profit rate.
- Bank Alfalah, Auto Loan (conventional): about 14.4% to 15.4% (KIBOR + 3.5% to 4.5%).
- HBL, CarLoan (conventional): about 14.5% to 16% (KIBOR + 3.5% to 5%).
- MCB Car4U, UBL Drive, Bank AL Habib (conventional) and BankIslami, Faysal Bank Car Ijarah (Islamic): market average about KIBOR + 2% to 4%.
- KIBOR (1-year offer rate) was 12.19% on 4 September 2026.

**Islamic vs conventional:** Islamic banks (Meezan, BankIslami, Faysal) use Ijarah (leasing) or Diminishing Musharakah instead of interest; conventional banks charge markup linked to KIBOR.

**Monthly instalment formula:** EMI = L × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where L is the loan, r the monthly rate and n the number of months. The Financing tab and the assistant's bank_financing tool apply the SBP rules automatically.

Also budget for insurance (usually 1.5–3% of the car's value per year), processing fees, registration and withholding tax.
