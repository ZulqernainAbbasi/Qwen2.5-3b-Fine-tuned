# Car Financing in Pakistan
Source: https://toyota-indus.com/buyers-guide

- Banks in Pakistan offer car financing (auto loans), usually for 1 to 7 years. The State Bank sets rules on the maximum loan amount and minimum down payment, so check current limits with your bank.
- **Down payment:** typically 30% or more of the car's price.
- **Markup (interest):** linked to KIBOR plus the bank's spread. Rates change often, so the assistant's EMI calculator uses 20% per year by default and lets you change it.
- **Toyota residual value financing:** Toyota allows up to 30% residual value financing for locally assembled Corolla, Yaris and Fortuner (for vehicles not older than 3 years), which lowers monthly instalments.
- **Booking tokens:** Toyota's buyer's guide lists partial-payment booking amounts such as Rs 1,500,000 for the Yaris and Rs 2,500,000 for the Corolla. KIA allowed Stonic Turbo bookings with Rs 1,000,000 partial payment for deliveries after December 2026.
- **Bank exchange programmes:** Suzuki has run exchange schemes with HBL for the Alto.

The EMI calculator uses standard amortisation: monthly instalment = L·r / (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where L is the loan, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.
