# Toyota Corolla Cross
Source: https://www.pakwheels.com/new-cars/toyota/corolla-cross/

Toyota's compact crossover with 1.8 petrol and 1.8 hybrid (HEV) options. Hybrid prices were cut on 13 Sep 2026.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 7,235,000 to Rs 9,729,000.

## Variants
- 1.8: Rs 7,235,000, 1798 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats, 12-16 km/l. Features: 7 airbags, rear AC vents, navigation, push start, climate control, cruise control
- 1.8 X: Rs 7,935,000, 1798 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats, 12-16 km/l
- 1.8 HEV: Rs 9,299,000, 1798 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats, 12-16 km/l
- 1.8 HEV X: Rs 9,729,000, 1798 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats, 12-16 km/l. Features: 7 airbags, power boot, rear AC vents, navigation, push start
