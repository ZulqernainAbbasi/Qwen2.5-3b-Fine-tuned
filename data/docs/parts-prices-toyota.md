# Toyota Corolla Maintenance and Parts Cost Estimates (Pakistan)
Source: Car Match AI, Toyota Corolla maintenance cost 2026 (https://carmatchai.blogspot.com/2026/04/toyota-corolla-maintenance-cost-in.html); Tyrepoint Corolla ownership guide (https://tyrepoint.pk/blog/the-real-cost-of-maintaining-a-toyota-corolla-in-pakistan-2023-2025)

These are estimated price ranges for genuine or OEM-quality parts, not fixed dealer prices.

**Toyota Corolla routine service (estimates, April 2026):**
- Engine oil change (5W-30): Rs 4,000 to Rs 6,000, every 5,000 km
- Oil filter: Rs 800 to Rs 1,200, at every oil change
- Air filter: Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,000, every 10,000 km
- Labour: Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per service
- Average maintenance: about Rs 8,000 to Rs 15,000 per month depending on usage

**Other Corolla parts (Tyrepoint estimates):**
- Coolant: Rs 2,000 to Rs 3,000, changed every 40,000 to 50,000 km
- Headlights (pair): Rs 12,000 to Rs 16,000 genuine, versus Rs 5,000 to Rs 7,000 Chinese/aftermarket

Toyota offers a 1-year prepaid periodic maintenance package at some dealers (Toyota Port Qasim Motors, 2025): four oil and oil-filter changes, a car wash and a health check at fixed prices. Confirm current prices with your Toyota dealer.
