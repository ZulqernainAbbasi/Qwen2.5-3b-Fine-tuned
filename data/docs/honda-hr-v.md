# Honda HR-V
Source: https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/25/honda-reduces-prices-of-2-hr-v-variants/

Compact crossover. The e:HEV is a two-motor hybrid. Both HR-V variants got limited-time price cuts on 13 Sep 2026.

Price range (ex-factory, Nov 2026): Rs 7,299,000 to Rs 9,299,000.

## Variants
- VTi-S: Rs 7,299,000, 1498 cc, petrol, automatic, 5 seats
- e:HEV: Rs 9,299,000, 1498 cc, hybrid, automatic, 5 seats. Features: two-motor hybrid
